# Community code of conduct

Usage Limit welcomes people of all experience levels and backgrounds.
English and Portuguese are both welcome.

- Be respectful. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Be patient with questions and first contributions.
- Keep feedback specific, constructive, and relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not share credentials, private messages, task transcripts,
  or identifying information without consent.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, spam, or solicit artificial engagement.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict access
when participation violates these expectations. Responses should be
proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Report abusive content through GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse**
tools. Do not disclose private details in public issues. Security vulnerabilities
belong in the [private reporting channel](SECURITY.md).
